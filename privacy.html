Privacy Policy

ATP Prep: FAA Airline Pilot for Windows · Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ATP Prep: FAA Airline Pilot for Windows (the “App”), published by MD Shahariar Nazim Joy (the “Developer”, “we”, “us”), handles information. The App is private by design.

1. We do not collect personal data

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. There are no accounts or logins. There is no analytics and no advertising.

2. Data stored on your device

Your study progress, quiz and mock results, bookmarks, streak and preferences (focus module, daily goal, optional test date) are stored only in the App's local storage on your Windows device. They never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall the App or reset progress in Settings.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store. We never see or receive payment card details. Microsoft's handling of purchase data is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

4. Network use

The App works offline. It connects to the internet only to download app updates, to let the Microsoft Store verify your purchases, and when you choose to open an external resource link (such as official FAA publications) in your browser.

5. No tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites. The App does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location access.

6. Children

The App is intended for pilots preparing for the FAA Airline Transport Pilot knowledge test (adults). It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: dmjks120@gmail.com.
